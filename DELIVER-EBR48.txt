Status
- The route, proportions, walk rule, colors and meshes are engineering
  placeholders, NOT Look Dev decisions or a final appearance. The provisional
  stream runs from the pond to the +X edge; a shallow wooden slab crosses it.
- Walking is blocked by stream and waterfall-lip water. The deck is temporarily
  `walkable`; changing the flag to `blocked` also blocks the deck and its
  overhang. Placement always rejects any overlapping 1.5-unit water or bridge
  tile, including the dry overhang of a walkable bridge.
- Convex footprints follow angled segments and a rotated bridge. A walkable
  deck removes only underlying water collision (including pond water if it
  overlaps); it does not erase a building, tree or fence collision. The player
  radius is respected. Water meshes, collision and placement use the same data.
- The bridge is a ground-level placeholder overlay, not new terrain or a new
  elevated walking surface. Existing island/stair support still limits walking.
  A future walkable deck must span the water and be wide enough for the
  character's 0.8-unit diameter, including clearance at both banks.

Existing code locations
- src/scene/createFarmLayout.ts: FARM_LAYOUT.pond, createPond's rim/water/depth
  meshes, and the original pond box with its 5% water-outline margin. The
  layout assembles building, tree, pond and new water/bridge obstacles.
- src/scene/createIsland.ts: 16 x 16 top, height 2.4, four cliff faces and
  underside. src/scene/createStoneStairs.ts supplies the existing stair opening
  and walkable support; neither terrain nor stairs was changed.
- src/placement/createPlacement.ts checks the shared obstacles and decoration
  bounds. src/placement/grid.ts defines TILE_SIZE = 1.5 and tests whole snapped
  fence/plot footprints; its only change adds polygon keep-out intersection.
- The cottage/coop collision definitions, dirt path, stairs, HUD, day/night,
  weather and pixel pass were not changed. In particular, the old rain-splash
  exclusion list remains unchanged; it does not yet exclude the new stream.

ONE table to edit
- src/scene/waterFeatureSpec.ts: WATER_FEATURE. All values in it are provisional.
  Positions and sizes are world units; Y lifts are relative to the island top.
  `bridge.length` runs along its local X axis, `bridge.width` across it, and
  `rotationDegrees` is a Three.js rotation about Y.
- `stream.points` can contain diagonal segments; the last point must lie on
  the selected waterfall side (east/west X = +/-8, north/south Z = -/+8).
  Adjacent points must differ and the waterfall width must fit on that side.
  `waterfall.width: null` inherits `stream.width`.
- src/scene/createWaterFeature.ts derives the placeholder meshes and named
  stream, waterfall-edge and wooden-bridge zones from that table;
  src/scene/farmFootprints.ts handles the convex footprint operations.

Exact outstanding Look Dev inputs
1. Stream route: each X/Z point, including pond junction and edge outlet;
   stream width and surface lift above the island.
2. Bridge: centre X/Z, length, width, Y rotation in degrees, deck thickness and
   top lift; choose `walkable` or `blocked`.
3. Waterfall: island side, width (or confirmation it equals the stream width),
   lip depth on the top and vertical drop height.
4. Hex colors for stream water, waterfall face and bridge wood. The current
   references to the existing farm palette only make the placeholders visible.
Check finalized values against the unchanged path, stairs, buildings and
island bounds; none of the provisional settings are a visual lock.
