Implementation
- src/scene/waterFeatureSpec.ts is the single locked data table. The sampled
  Catmull-Rom stream has 1.5 rim, 1.2 water, 0.35 deep stripe and alternating
  0.3 x 0.1 foam. The +Z waterfall has the 1.2 x 0.2 lip, a 3.6-high lit
  sheet 0.06 in front of the rim, and a 0.8-high alpha fade to y=-1.2.
- The 2.0 x 1.4 bridge has five 0.4 planks, four 0.04 gap lines and two
  three-post rails. Both ends grade over 0.3 to the 0.12-high top; the walking
  capsule and its shadow follow the visible surface without a step.
- Stream boxes block walking; their portions beneath the full bridge deck are
  removed. The original pond box remains untouched. The deck is walkable but
  reserves its entire footprint for 1.5-unit placement tiles. Rain excludes
  the uncut stream boxes and full deck, in addition to the original shelters.
- Engineer: source collision boxes are about one unit long, each with the
  locked 0.63 half-width and a 0.05 margin at each end; paired boxes cover curved
  segments. Shorter samples at the bridge approaches prevent a false wall on
  the existing path. The two listed grass tufts alone are hidden; every other
  instance retains its transform.
- Engineer: waterfall streaks advance in 0.1-unit steps every 0.4 seconds,
  averaging the locked 0.25 units/second. Tilted normals and a 2x reflected-
  light response keep the sheet readable while it follows existing scene
  lighting. Its locked colors are retained; it has no emissive and adds no
  lights. Day/night, weather, path, stairs, buildings, trees, HUD and grid
  implementation remain unchanged. Click-to-move still does not pathfind: a
  straight shortcut through the new stream stops, but the dirt path via the
  bridge and the stairs are traversable.

   z=-2.5025, so it occupies z=-2.65..-2.5025 inside the requested gap at
   x=3.3..4.5: 0.1475 deep. Stream boxes themselves do not enter the deck.
   At bridge center z=-1.95 the 0.4-radius character still has 0.1525 pond
   clearance, and real click/WASD tests cross. Keeping the pond box and making
   the entire stated gap water-obstacle-free cannot both be satisfied.
2. West keep-out: the locked route/width gives water min x=2.2322, rim min
   x=2.0822 and a collision-box min x=2.2054, all west of x=2.9. The new
   feature's max x=4.9, so the x>5.2 stair keep-out is clear. Actual closest
   X/Z distance from water to coop B roof is 2.0671 (passes 0.8); comparing
   their global X envelopes alone gives only 0.0322. Clarify if the 0.8 rule
   was intended as an X-only gap rather than real planar distance.
3. Deck versus unchanged dirt: at x=4.9 the dark path border reaches
   z=-1.0241, 0.2259 beyond the deck's z=-1.25 edge. At the graded ends the
   ground-flush deck is also below the existing +0.020 border/+0.024 surface.
   Thus the locked deck cannot completely cover the real border, although the
   path and bridge are walkable end to end.
4. Existing nest at (3.2, 1.2): its center is 0.2356 from the stream
   centerline, 0.3644 inside the 0.6 water half-width; its X/Z bounds are
   2.91..3.49 / 0.91..1.49. The nest is unchanged and visibly overlaps water.
5. Minimum feature size: the locked 0.04 plank gap line is 0.06 below the
   stated 0.1 minimum. The specified gap is retained rather than widened.
6. Junction observation: at x=3.9 the unchanged pond's inner water ends at
   z=-2.5921 while the locked stream starts at z=-2.45, leaving 0.1421 between
