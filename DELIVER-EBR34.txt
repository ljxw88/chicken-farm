Run and walk
- `npm install && npm run dev`; open the URL Vite prints. The current production preview is http://127.0.0.1:5195/ while its process is running.
- From spawn, walk up the tan path with screen-relative WASD or click/tap successive points on the path. At the fork, follow the left branch past the trees to the X-braced barn door. Return to the fork and take the right branch to the pond. The camera follows throughout.
- For a longer look, use `setDayNightCycleSeconds(3600); setDayNightPhase('daytime')` in the browser console; use `setDayNightPhase('night')` to see both barn window lights and the carried lantern. The existing 72-second cycle remains the default.

Collision and look
- The barn's rectangular footprint, four tree trunks and the pond water are solid for both WASD and click/tap movement. The circular character footprint is included, movement is substepped to prevent tunnelling, and diagonal movement can slide along obstacles. A blocked click stops when it cannot advance; it does not pathfind. The pond uses a conservative circular blocker around its water.
- All farm colors are provisional pending Look Dev lock and are centralized in `src/scene/farmPalette.ts`: grass #7CB070; path #C4A06A; walls #C89070, roof #A05048, door #6B4A3A; trunk #6B4A30, foliage #4A8A50; water #5A9AB8, wet edge #8A7060.
- The existing camera, locked day/night presets and postFX were not changed. The former shed stubs were replaced by the barn and its two original-style warm PointLights.
