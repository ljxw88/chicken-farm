Visible-change ledger and design values
- Smoke originates at cottage-chimney-top, world (-1.9,6.0,-4.1). It has six
  detail-0, flat-shaded icosahedra with base 0xefe3c8 and bounding width
  0.28 -> 0.65 (growth exponent 1.5); it rises at 0.45 u/s with unchanged wind
  (+0.235094, 0, -0.085034) u/s. Life is 3.6 s, or 2.6 s in rain. Peak
  opacity is 0.75, held through 90% of life then eased to zero with
  1 - smoothstep(progress, 0.9, 1)^4. Rain and night
  each reduce it to 60%; together they use a single 60% factor rather than
  compounding. Birth bounds start at y=5.86, 0.36 above the roof ridge; the
  geometry test finds no puff/roof or puff/window intersection.
- Smoke remains scene-lit with black emissive and no new lights. A smoke-only
  9x reflected-light response, uniformly capped by its albedo, improves
  legibility under the signed scene lighting; a lights-off/fog-off rendered
  check recorded zero brightened pixels.
- Fireflies use one unlit instanced draw, 0.22 x 0.22 world-unit yellow cores
  (0xf2e27a), no halo and no lights. Stable night counts are 14 clear, 10
  cloudy, 6 rain; morning/day/sunset anchors are zero. Pulses span 0.25–1.0
  over 2.5–4.5 s, rain survivors are multiplied by 0.6, and phase/weather
  envelopes crossfade. Closed 3-D Lissajous loops have horizontal radii
  0.5–1.19, centre heights 0.63–1.47 above grass, and 3-D speed within the
  requested 0.15–0.35 u/s.
  Seeded homes sit near trees, stream and pond; loops and their projected quads
  stay inside the island and away from building roofs and the stair rim.
- In the recorded 1280x720 frames the firefly-channel maxima are RGB

Spec tensions / omissions
- Six slots, 0.55 s birth ticks and a 3.6 s life cannot all be exact: seven
  puffs would overlap for 0.30 s. A birth is skipped when all six slots remain
  alive, preserving their life and the cap. Clear/cloudy settle at 5–6 live
  puffs, averaging six accepted births per 3.85 s (about 0.642 s); the 2.6 s
  rain life yields 4–5. The initial plume is prewarmed at 0.55 s intervals.
- Fireflies are exactly zero at the sunset phase anchor. The existing 72 s
  cycle interpolates sunset to night over 18 s while the HUD still says Sunset;
  at its 9 s midpoint the night amount is 0.5 and the motes are at half opacity.
  This preserves the requested continuous night-amount fade instead of popping
  at the phase-label boundary. Morning/day anchors remain zero.
- The optional 1.2-unit dynamic player clearance is not enforced; the effects
  remain nonblocking and do not couple their motion to walking or placement.
