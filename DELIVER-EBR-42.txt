Visible changes
- Fireflies remain the signed, non-emissive instanced effect: 14 at clear
  night, 10 in cloudy night and 6 in rainy night; zero at morning/day/sunset
  anchors. No firefly source, light, colour or halo value changed.
- Chimney smoke holds peak opacity through 40% of life, then fades using
  1 - smoothstep(progress, 0.4, 1). Each birth has a seeded size factor in
  [0.85, 1.15] and seeded-phase lateral sway of at most +/-0.06 world units
  about its existing wind path (four-second period). The existing RNG draw is
  split so the previous quarter-turn orientation stream stays unchanged.
- The six flat, scene-lit, non-emissive puffs retain base colour 0xefe3c8,
  nominal 0.28 -> 0.65 growth, 0.45 u/s rise, wind, 0.75 peak opacity, and
  3.6 s life (2.6 s in rain). Rain and night each dim to 60%, without double
  dimming. Their 9x capped reflected-light response and firefly code are unchanged.
- Six slots, a 3.6 s life and 0.55 s scheduled births require seven overlapping
  puffs for 0.30 s; a birth is skipped when full rather than cutting a life.
  The optional 1.2-unit player clearance remains unenforced. Effects are
  nonblocking and do not alter walking, placement, picking, HUD or lighting.
