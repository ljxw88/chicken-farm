Spec tensions / omissions
- Five slots, 0.6 s births and a 3.2 s life cannot all be exact: a sixth puff
  would overlap for 0.2 s. A scheduled birth is skipped when all five slots
  remain alive, preserving each life and the cap. Clear/cloudy settle at 4–5
  live puffs, with five births per 3.6 s (0.72 s average); a 2.2 s rain life at
  the same 0.6 s schedule yields 3–4 live puffs, not five. The initial plume
  is prewarmed at ages 0, 0.6, 1.2, 1.8 and 2.4 s so it is visible immediately.
- Phase-anchor sunset is exactly zero. The existing 72 s cycle interpolates
  sunset to night over 18 s while the HUD still says Sunset; at its 9 s midpoint
  the existing night amount is 0.5 and the motes are faded to half opacity.
  This preserves the requested continuous night-amount fade instead of popping
  at the phase-label boundary. Morning/day anchors remain zero.
- The optional 1.2-unit dynamic player clearance is not enforced; the effects
  remain nonblocking and do not couple their motion to walking or placement.
