Run and QA
- Use Node 20.19+ or 22.12+: `npm ci && npm run dev`, then open the printed local URL. The production version can be checked with `npm run build && npm run preview`.
- At spawn, the small white chicken is screen-down from the character; its egg is to the chicken's right. Hold S until the "Feed chicken (stub)" prompt appears. Release S and press E, click the chicken, or click/tap the prompt: "Fed chicken (QA stub). Feed: 0." appears.
- Move right toward the egg (hold S+D from the chicken until "Collect egg (stub)" appears). Press E or click/tap the egg or prompt. The egg disappears and the feedback says "Collected egg (QA stub). Eggs: 1." The egg is present from the start; feeding does not instantly create it.
- The QA seed is one free feed and one visible egg per page load. A second feed attempt reports no feed. Refresh to reset; neither count persists. Feeding is not a purchase and collection is not a sale.
- The bin by the barn opens and closes with its prompt; it does not provide storage or trade. Talk to the sign beside the path for stub feedback, including the supplied EBR-54 examples: egg sale +3 coins and feed purchase -1 coin. These are informational only; no coin balance, purchase, sale or other unprovided rate is implemented.
- Entering or crossing an interaction radius only shows a prompt. E is edge-triggered (release before the next press); holding E while entering does not activate. An out-of-range object click/tap is ordinary click-to-move, not a queued interaction: explicitly interact again after approaching. A successful interaction cancels any remaining click-to-move destination, but does not cancel held WASD. Ground clicks, screen-relative WASD, camera following and farm collision geometry are otherwise unchanged.

Integration and dependencies
- `src/interactions/Interactable.ts` defines an id, world object, prompt label, ground-plane range, optional prompt height, optional explicit click targets, availability predicate and primary action with optional plain-text feedback. Register with `createInteractionController(...).register(interactable)`; its returned function unregisters it. Moving objects are located in world coordinates every frame. Nearest available object gets the E/prompt action; a direct click chooses the hit object and rechecks range and availability. Decorative shadows can be excluded via `clickTargets`.
- Static flock/inventory stand-ins are isolated in `src/interactions/qa/createQaFlockStubs.ts`; bin/sign stand-ins are separate in `createQaWorldStubs.ts`. Each has cleanup and availability guards. Remove/unregister the stubs when real systems are wired in; no flock behavior is implied.
- EBR-58 flock/AI must supply real chickens, feeding eligibility/effects, egg spawning/lifecycle and appropriate registration/unregistration. The later inventory ticket must replace the ephemeral QA feed and egg counts with actual inventory and persistence. Later economy/spend and storage/dialogue tickets must provide the real UI, balance checks and transactions using the complete authoritative signed EBR-54 rate table; no additional rates were inferred here.
- No diorama rebuild, glass HUD panel or pixel post-processing was added.
