Nest READY steps and expected results
1. Open the preview. The white chicken is screen-down from spawn, and its egg is to the right. Hold S toward the chicken, then release when "Feed chicken (stub)" appears. Just entering range must not feed it or show action feedback.
2. Press E once or click/tap the prompt. Expect the brief message "Fed chicken (QA stub). Feed: 0." Release E between presses. Another attempt reports "No feed left (QA stub). Refresh to reset."
3. Move toward the egg (S+D from the chicken works), stop when "Collect egg (stub)" appears, and press E or click/tap the prompt. The egg disappears and the brief message reads "Collected egg (QA stub). Eggs: 1."
4. Walk/click toward the small bin in front of the barn until "Open feed bin (stub)" appears. Press E or click/tap it: the lid opens and the message says storage and trade are not connected. Use the "Close feed bin (stub)" prompt to close it. From a refreshed spawn, W approaches the bin.
5. Walk/click toward the wooden sign on the path to the right of the barn. Use "Talk to sign (stub)". The message states "egg sale +3 coins; feed purchase -1 coin" and that trading is not connected.
6. Walking through an interaction area without E or an explicit object/prompt click must not trigger an action. Clicking an object from too far away only walks toward the clicked ground; it does not queue interaction. WASD and camera following remain unchanged.

The chicken, egg, bin and sign are QA stubs. A page refresh resets the one free feed and one egg. Feeding is not a purchase; collecting is not a sale. The rates are informational only; no balance or trade transaction is implemented.
