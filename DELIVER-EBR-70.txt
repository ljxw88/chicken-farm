Merge wiring
- EBR-75's engine owns the day/night and weather entities, their full-frame
  systems, the placement adapter and the original update ordering. The lighting
  adapter shares the engine day/night state; its existing night amount continues
  to drive the unchanged sheep wool shader through the existing key light.
- Both farm and sheepfold shadows receive the same phase/weather callback.
  Sheep animation updates after the existing waterfall update on each frame.
- The nine fence boxes, shelter box, two radius-0.4 sheep circles and the
  non-walking placement reservation enter the shared obstacle array before the
  character controller mirrors them into Miniplex obstacle entities. The
  reservation blocks placement, not walking.
- The sheepfold geometry, materials, palette, wool limiter, lights, shelter,
  collision dimensions, seed and all other look constants remain unchanged.
  See evidence/EBR-70/merge-6daf042/README.md for the new checks and captures.
- The original EBR-75 real-time QA result in DELIVER-EBR75.txt was not passing;
  the deterministic merge comparisons do not turn that historical result into

  The 4.5 × 3.0 reservation is X [-6.95,-2.45], Z [3.1,6.1]. Reused fence

  rendered fence bounds are 4.60 × 2.97. The nearest pre-existing collider is

  (X,Y,Z) are 0.580 × 0.550 × 0.890 and 0.585 × 0.550 × 0.890, inside the
  revision's 0.55–0.60 × 0.50–0.55 × 0.85–0.90 ranges. Rotation gives larger
  world-axis AABBs; see the revision geometry.json. Overall height is 0.760.
- The brown face now measures 0.36 × 0.32 × 0.36, versus the original approx.
  0.30 × 0.30 × 0.32. It protrudes 0.348 and 0.342 units at the seeded pose;
  the 1°-sampled nod range has a 0.335 minimum, above the 0.25 requirement.
  The legs are 0.24 × 0.32 ×
  0.24 rather than the original 0.12 × 0.20 × 0.12, exposing 0.210 below the
  wool and making them readable at the actual pixel pass. One rear leg of the
  second sheep is staggered 0.08 units; ears are 0.12 × 0.12 × 0.14 versus the

  Wool materials use only #fff1d7, #f3ead6 and #e4d1ae. A wool-only highlight
  limiter still caps linear outgoing light at 0.9 (~243 sRGB). A wool-only
  response to the existing blue-biased moon key blends toward linear RGB gains
  (1.60,1.00,0.70), warming night shading without changing the albedo tokens,
  daytime/sunset shader response, or scene lighting.

  its 0.12 overhang gives a measured 1.64 × 1.44 roof footprint. It is a solid
  collision box despite its open-looking front, as specified.
