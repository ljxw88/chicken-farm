Measured design approximations / deviations
- The nominal center is (-4.7, 4.6), shifted +0.5 X from the requested center.
  The 4.5 × 3.0 reservation is X [-6.95,-2.45], Z [3.1,6.1]. Reused fence
  collision envelopes extend 0.05 X and 0.03 Z beyond those nominal edges:
  X [-7.0,-2.4], Z [3.07,6.13] (4.60 × 3.06). The nearest rim gap is 1.00;
  rendered fence bounds are 4.60 × 2.97. The nearest pre-existing collider is
  tree 1 at 1.236 units; the conservative path-bounding-box gap is 1.213. Corner
  posts are shared rather than duplicated to avoid coplanar fence meshes.
- Measured wool-body bounds (X,Y,Z) are 0.590 × 0.635 × 0.960 and
  0.625 × 0.615 × 0.970, versus the approximate 0.6 × 0.55 × 0.9 design. The
  grounded overall heights are 0.790 and 0.780 versus 0.8; leg stubs are exactly
  0.12 × 0.20 × 0.12. Individual lobe diameters are about 0.54–0.58 versus
  the approximate 0.50–0.55 design. The taller overlapping wool keeps the feet joined.
- Both face materials use #6b4a2e; the second uses a 0.86 linear vertex-colour
  multiplier for the requested slightly darker head, without a new palette hue.
  Wool materials use only #fff1d7, #f3ead6 and #e4d1ae. A wool-only highlight
  limiter caps linear outgoing light at 0.9 (~243 sRGB) without changing albedo
  tokens or scene lighting, preventing clipped-white wool across phases.
- The shelter body is 1.4 × 1.2, top 1.1, rise 0.3 over its 1.2 depth, and
  its 0.12 overhang gives a measured 1.64 × 1.44 roof footprint. It is a solid
  collision box despite its open-looking front, as specified.
