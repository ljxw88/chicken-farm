Run and toggle
- On carrier, the production preview is http://100.88.150.21:5196/ (Tailscale), or http://127.0.0.1:5195/ locally, while those preview processes are running. In a fresh QA checkout, use `npm ci && npm run build && npm run preview -- --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5200 --strictPort`, then open http://127.0.0.1:5200/.
- Weather starts clear. In the browser console use `setWeather('rain')` and `setWeather('clear')`; `getWeather()` returns `{ mode, rainAmount }`, where `mode` is the requested state and `rainAmount` smoothly ranges from 0 to 1. Invalid modes throw a RangeError.
- For inspection, `setDayNightCycleSeconds(36000); setDayNightPhase('daytime')` holds the day nearly still; `setDayNightPhase('night')` shows the warm windows and lantern. The normal day/night cycle continues independently.
- In a 1280×720 browser open the console, run `setDayNightCycleSeconds(36000); setDayNightPhase('daytime'); setWeather('clear')`, wait 2 seconds, then `setWeather('rain')`. Wait another 2 seconds and inspect the streaks/splashes; `getWeather()` should report rainAmount 1. Run `setWeather('clear')`, wait 2 seconds and confirm rainAmount 0 and the restored lighting. Repeat the same sequence after `setDayNightPhase('night')`; the two warm windows and lantern should remain lit.
- For gameplay QA, hold WASD and click/tap along the path toward the barn and pond while raining. Push into the barn and tree trunks and confirm the character stops; try the pond and confirm it remains blocked. Run `setDayNightCycleSeconds(12)` and toggle weather repeatedly as phases progress.

Look Dev visually approved starting targets
- `src/weather/rainSettings.ts` centralizes the visually approved starting settings: sparse 240 unblurred line streaks, about 15° on screen, #B8CFDA at 0.35 opacity. Their world-space length produces roughly 3.8–4.8 px at 1280×720; the future 1×3–5 px pixel-resolution intent is not enforced with postFX.
- Occasional thin ground rings expand from radius 0.08 to 0.18 and fade over 0.25–0.4 s, with at most six active across the field. Exclusions cover the barn roof and its overhang, tree crowns, pond water and ground edges; no dense pond ripples are added.
- A full clear↔rain blend takes 1.75 s, including smooth reversal mid-transition. At full rain the directional intensity is 80% of the current interpolated day/night intensity; clear restores that exact current-phase value without cumulative scaling. Phase colors, ambient/hemisphere lighting, fog, ground material and warm practical lights remain unchanged. No lightning, heavy haze, glossy ground, HUD or postFX was added.
