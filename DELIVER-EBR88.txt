Visible-change ledger
- Rain streak length is 0.40–0.60 world units (full 3-D line length), formerly
  0.075–0.095 vertical units. They remain unblurred, one low-resolution pixel
  wide, and use the existing line primitive.
- The 15-degree screen tilt now leans down-right toward world (+0.94, 0, -0.34),
  and the moving drops drift in that same world direction.
- Rain tint is 0xd6e8f2, formerly 0xb8cfda; full opacity is 0.55, formerly
  0.35. Splash materials share the rain tint, so existing rings are also tinted.
- The player-following rain field is 19 x 19 x 8, formerly 24 x 24 x 10.
  One shared width controls both horizontal axes; no separate depth setting was
  needed. At spawn the 16 x 16 plateau has a 1.5-unit margin on each side.
  Horizontal density increases by about 1.60x at the same 240-streak budget;
  impact locations and timing also change as the field and drift change.
- Engineer decision: no optional void clipping/fade or ring-size retune was
  added. Existing 0.08–0.18 splash radii, capacity 6, chance, lifetime and
  roof/pond/stream/deck exclusions remain as before. Rain can still be visible
  over the void, especially as the field follows the player.
- Vertical fall speeds remain 4–5 units/s and the transition remains 1.75 seconds.
  No lights were added; particle budget, clear/cloudy weather, lighting, pixel
  pass and HUD code are unchanged.

Conflicts for Look Dev
- The spec's world direction is not the current contact-shadow direction.
  Normalized rain is (+0.94038, 0, -0.34014), while main's key-aligned contact
  shadow is (+0.8, 0, -0.6): a measured 16.98-degree difference in the ground
  plane. Engineer kept the specified rain vector and did not change lighting.
- The spec references grass around RGB (70,119,49). Main at pixel (320,360)
  measures (71,119,50) in clear and (61,105,44) in rain: the existing rain
  lighting darkens it. Engineer retained the specified tint and lighting.
- With the actual isometric camera and exact 3-D lengths, projected streak
  heights are approximately 3.07–4.60 low-resolution pixels before rasterizing,
  versus the spec's approximate 3–4.5 pixels. The stated lengths are retained.
- The unchanged 4–5 units/s setting is vertical speed. Including horizontal
  drift, actual 3-D speed is 4.13–5.17 units/s, versus 4.09–5.12 on main.
  Engineer retained the existing vertical speeds rather than retuning them.
- A full browser run sampled sunset rain at shade/lit 0.7375 against the 0.75
  lighting threshold. A same-frame native-resolution diagnostic found 15 of
  120 unmasked frames below 0.75, worst 0.7213; with rain weather still active
  and particles hidden, every paired frame measured 0.77236. Engineer kept the
  prescribed rain and all lighting thresholds, adding an opt-in, test-only rain
  mask to the lighting probe. Actual rain remains in the visual evidence; the
  unmasked test failure and diagnostic are retained for Look Dev review.
- HUD DOM, layout, styling and isolated pixels are identical. Its existing
  translucent backdrop reflects rain behind it, so the raw rain frames have
  1,291 changed pixels inside HUD bounds by day and 1,326 by night. Engineer
  left the HUD untouched; exact composited HUD pixels cannot remain identical
  while those background rain pixels change.
