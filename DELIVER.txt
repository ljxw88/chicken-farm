Root cause and fix
- W+A is screen-diagonal but moves along world -X. After a side push the
  character's circular footprint hit the concave pad-to-tread junction. The
  axis-only collision fallback had no world-Z input to slide around that corner.
  The previous browser check recentered on the pad before climbing, masking it.
- At that junction only, blocked inward movement uses its unspent distance to
  slide toward the narrow stair opening. The existing support and obstacle
  checks still constrain each substep; pad edges and the void remain solid.
  The fix does not alter tread geometry, pad size, colors, lighting or camera.
