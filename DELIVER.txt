Scope and timing
- Miniplex 2.0.0 is pinned; project IDs are stable and components are plain data.
  The fixed 1/60 s simulation runs ordered systems with 0.1 s maximum catch-up;
  one-shot actions are consumed once per tick and held movement persists.
- No gameplay system has been ported. Character, collision/stairs, placement,
  lighting, weather, HUD and rendering remain in their existing paths and order.
  Walking still gets min(frame delta, 0.1); lighting/weather/render get the
  full frame delta. Action listeners observe without consuming legacy events.
- Rain already accepted an optional RNG. The parity harness supplies its seeded
  RNG when constructing weather, before the initial particles; the default stays
  Math.random. It drives real click/WASD/placement paths at controlled frame
  times and checks the camera/pose and pinned phase/weather.
