Diagnosis / Engineer decisions
- Main's sun swung from +X to +Z to -X; ambient/hemisphere fill was too strong
  for the key. The night moon and a 0.55 front-mounted lantern further filled
  the face. The character was non-emissive, outward-facing Standard material;
  the pixel pass did not tint or invert shading. Main had no shadow maps: its
  centred blob even brightened dark ground at sunset/night.
- One key now remains at world (-11,h,4), target origin, across all phases;
  height, light color/intensity and smaller fill vary. The lantern is off by day,
  0.08 at night (range 2), while its visible glass stays at its prior glow.
  A non-emissive character tint/top lift improves separation and facing stability.
- Character, trees, cottage, both coops, chicken, bridge and waterfall lip use
  black-alpha offset ground blobs opposite that same key; cloud/rain soften them.
  The player blob stays on stairs/bridge support. No shadow maps were added.
  The path's seven inverted triangles and the subpixel lantern wing were fixed
  separately with before/after evidence. Signed farm/water/building/ghost hexes,
  #101416 void, HUD and 4-CSS-pixel presentation remain intact.
