Where the old barn was
- src/scene/createFarmLayout.ts had FARM_LAYOUT.barn, createBarn(), its
  walking obstacle and the rain-splash roof exclusion. This base contained one
  barn, not three; there were no existing coop locations.
- src/scene/farmPalette.ts supplies the old barn colors; fence visuals in
  src/placement/createPlacementVisual.ts and stone in
  src/scene/createStoneStairs.ts also use those colors. They are unchanged.
- src/lighting/createPracticalLights.ts parents window lights to barn panes;
  its lantern is carried by the character, not anchored to a barn.
- src/scene/farmCollisions.ts moves against the layout's obstacles, and
  src/placement/createPlacement.ts rejects whole ghost footprints against
  those same obstacles.

What changed
- Removed the barn mesh. Added a cottage box and two named ramp-coop boxes.
  They abut and partition the original barn's footprint, rather than inventing
  two new locations. These are deliberately plain boxes, not final buildings:
  there are no modeled ramps, roof, white finish or approved new colors yet.
- Meshes, named walking/placement obstacles and rain exclusions are derived
  from the same table. The cottage's two flush window panes and their light
  anchors scale and move with the cottage. Night intensities are unchanged;
  the lantern remains attached to the moving character as before.
- No terrain, stream, HUD, grid, stair or path implementation changes. The
  existing staircase and fence palette were not modified.
