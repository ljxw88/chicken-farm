Nest on-screen gate
1. Refresh: the top-right capsule says Supplies 0 and the open Growing on your farm list shows Hen coop, 1 ready, a checked circle, next-egg timer placeholder, and +5 Supplies. Day 1, Spring and Woodland are stubs; the phase follows the lighting cycle.
2. From spawn, hold S toward the white chicken and release when Feed chicken (stub) appears. Hold S+D toward the egg and release when Collect egg (stub) appears; if you pass it, step back. On a small screen, collapse Growing to uncover the scene. Ground clicks/taps also walk but never auto-collect.
3. Press E, click/tap the prompt, or click/tap the egg itself while in range. The egg disappears, Supplies goes 0 -> 5, and the row changes to 0 ready / Collected with a placeholder ring. The same egg cannot be collected again.
4. Refresh, then use the Growing list's Collect button from spawn. It intentionally works at any distance and removes the very same world egg; Supplies again goes 0 -> 5. Walk to the former egg position: there is no second egg or second Supplies award.
5. Click + Build: a placeholder sheet opens, with no full Build menu. Close with the X. Spring growth and Bee pollination expose placeholder text; no buff is applied.

Scope / rate
- One QA egg awards exactly +5 Supplies once, whether collected in-world or from the list. The +5 is not a coin sale and does not implement the EBR-55 sign's informational egg-sale rate. The seed and counter are in memory and reset on refresh.
- New eggs, real timers, buff simulation, flock/economy transactions, persistent inventory and the full EBR-66 Build menu are not implemented. Day/season/biome are static. EBR-36 sky-panel internals and the world renderer were not changed.
- The HUD root is an HTML sibling of the canvas, not part of the pixel/nearest pass. The ready ring changes to a no-timer placeholder after collection; the list, capsule and world share the guarded egg action.
